# Call of Cthulhu: edition and sheet map

This is a **Call of Cthulhu 7th edition** guest investigator sheet. Investigators
follow clues and confront horror; the Keeper controls the setting, asks for
checks and adjudicates consequences. Ordinary investigators are vulnerable:
combat success is not the only way to resolve a mystery. The plugin does not
provide scenarios, paid rulebooks or Pulp Cthulhu character automation.
See the [official introduction](https://cthulhuwiki.chaosium.com/rules/).

| Sheet area | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Profile | Occupation, age, era, pronouns and places |
| Characteristics | STR, CON, SIZ, DEX, APP, INT, POW and EDU |
| Skills and weapons | Percentile values, specializations and weapon skill links |
| Resources | Current HP, MP, Sanity and Luck; derived limits are calculated |
| Backstory | Beliefs, relationships, significant places/objects and history |
| Inventory and notes | Equipment and Keeper-approved campaign facts |

The sheet calculates derived values and supports populated creation, batches of
1–20 and revisions of attached snapshots. It does not allocate occupation budgets,
adjust characteristics for age or randomly generate a rules-legal investigator.
Assigned values are **custom builds** unless separately verified against the chosen
creation procedure. A schema-valid payload alone is not rules validation.

The [official investigator guide](https://cthulhuwiki.chaosium.com/investigators/)
is a simplified creation route. Keep it distinct from the full Keeper Rulebook/
Investigator Handbook procedure. Use [creation](character-creation.md) to turn a
concept into supported inputs, and [workflows](workflows.md) before sending them.
