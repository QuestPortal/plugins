Investigator Character Sheet

Your Call of Cthulhu 7th edition investigator, ready for the next mystery.

Create and edit a guest character sheet without an account. Track characteristics, skills, health, sanity, luck, weapons, backstory, and possessions. Roll percentile checks and weapon damage in the sheet or through supported chat tools.

Your draft stays with you

Drafts stay in your browser where storage is supported. Export JSON for a portable backup and import it on another device. Explicitly attach a draft when you want to share its values with your chat assistant. Opening the sheet does not automatically let the assistant read your local drafts.

Play together

Optional private sessions share dice events with explicitly subscribed chats on hosts that support MCP Events. Sessions expire after 24 hours and retain up to 1,000 rolls. Keep session tokens private.

This guest service has no payments or purchases. New investigators are editable starting points, not complete rules-generated characters. Rolls do not automatically apply damage or spend luck.
