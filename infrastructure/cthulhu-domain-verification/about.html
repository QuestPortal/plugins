A few things to know

Where is my investigator saved?

Drafts stay in this browser where local storage is supported. There is no cloud investigator library or automatic device sync. Clearing browser data can remove drafts, so export JSON to keep a backup.

Can my chat assistant see my sheet?

Only when you explicitly share it, such as by attaching your draft or exported JSON. Opening the sheet does not automatically let the assistant read your browser-local drafts. Proposed chat edits do not automatically change your sheet.

Can I share rolls with my group’s chat?

Optional private play sessions can send dice events to explicitly subscribed chats on hosts that support MCP Events. Sessions expire after 24 hours and retain up to 1,000 rolls. Keep the session token private: anyone holding it can access or end that session.

Does it create a complete, rules-ready character?

New drafts are editable starting points. Complete character creation with your Keeper and the game’s rules. Rolls do not automatically apply damage, spend luck, or change sheet resources.
