Support

Help with your Call of Cthulhu guest sheet.

Email hi@questportal.com for support or privacy questions. Include your chat host or browser, what you tried, and the visible error. Use a synthetic example where possible. Do not send session tokens, passwords, or unrelated private campaign content.

Where is my investigator?

Drafts remain in the browser where they were created, when storage is supported. There is no server-side investigator library. Use JSON export and import to move drafts. Clearing browser data can remove drafts; export a backup before clearing it.

Can chat read or change my sheet?

Share a draft using Attach or an exported JSON file. Chat cannot automatically retrieve browser-local drafts. Proposed chat edits do not automatically change your sheet.

How do I stop sharing rolls?

Disconnect the sheet to stop its future roll sharing. End the private session to clear active retained rolls, subscriptions, and pending events. Delivered messages remain subject to the destination chat host’s controls.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No. This guest plugin requires no Quest Portal account and has no payments or purchases.
